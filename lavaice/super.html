Field guide

Stay alive on the lavice.

Survive as long as you can. Five minutes is the mark the Superbot is built to beat.

Controls

WASD
Move.
←↑↓→
Hold to fire. Two arrows together fire diagonally.
Space
Dash in the direction you are moving (straight up if standing still). Recharges in under a second.
E
Lay the landmine where you stand, once the Mine meter reads Ready.
P R G
Pause · restart · this guide.
C
Change costume: default, alien, ninja, melon, black hole, orca, clock, donut. Looks only.
B 1–4
Hand over to the Superbot (the run stops counting as yours) · game speed 1× to 8×.

What hurts

  • Lavice — the glowing blue masses with a molten rim. Pressing against the rim burns you; being inside one slows you to a crawl and burns about 30 integrity a second. It drifts constantly: the dashed line shows where the rim is heading.
  • Lava debris — slow, takes 5 hits, 400 points. You walk more than twice as fast as it does.
  • Ice dust — almost as fast as you, dies to 1 hit, 150 points. Shoot these first.
  • Any touch costs 10 integrity and knocks you back. You regain about 3 a second.
  • The map edge — pinned against it you cannot be knocked clear, so one enemy hits again and again and a full bar is gone in a blink.

Your tools

  • Dash — passes through lavice and through enemies without harm. Do not end a dash inside lavice or beyond the edge of the map: out there you are frozen and burning.
  • Landmine — ready 30 seconds after the last one; at 60 seconds it is laid for you. Each wave it recharges a little faster (down to 12 seconds) and reaches a little further. Enemies within its outer ring abandon you and go for the mine. The first to touch it lights a short fuse, then everything inside the dashed ring dies. Only one exists at a time, lavice covering it for 3 seconds destroys it, the blast cannot hurt you, and its kills score nothing.
  • Gun — shots stop at lavice. Every 1,200 points raises the wave, and each wave makes enemies arrive faster (past wave 20, by a little less each time).

How to last

  • Keep moving through open ground; corners and narrow pockets are where runs end.
  • Shooting lava debris is a trade: points now, a faster swarm for the rest of the run. The Superbot's default is to kill only ice dust and outrun the rest.
  • Lay the mine when a pack is close behind you, then walk away — it will pull them in.
  • Save the dash for getting out, not for getting around.
